Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Radex Loader is an internal tool operated by Radex Import & Marketing Ltd. ("Radex").

What the app accesses

With the authorization of the Radex company Google account, the app reads sales report files placed in designated Radex folders in that account's Google Drive, and moves processed files between those folders. It does not access any other files, email, contacts or personal Google account data.

How the data is used

The files are used only to produce internal sales reports for authorized Radex staff. The data is stored on servers controlled by Radex.

Sharing

Radex does not sell, share or transfer this data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

Google API data

Radex Loader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Radex Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
